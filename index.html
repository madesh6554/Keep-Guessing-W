How to play

  1. Say one word. The next word must start with the last letter of the word before. cat → tiger → rabbit → tulip
  2. No repeats. A word can only be used once per game.
  3. Beat the clock. If time runs out, or you pass, you lose a life. Lose all your lives and you are out.
  4. Last player standing wins. With a word limit, the top score wins.

Rules you can switch on

Every 5th word grows. Word 5 needs 6+ letters, word 10 needs 7+, word 15 needs 8+, and it keeps climbing.

Category. Every word must fit a theme like Animals or Food. If no unused theme word starts with the needed letter, any word counts for that turn.

Speed up. The timer drops by one second every 10 words, down to 4 seconds.

No proper names. Names of people, places and brands are rejected.

Scoring

10 points per letter, plus 3 per second left on the clock, plus 50 for a growing-word challenge. Keep a streak going for up to double points.

Typing tip: the starting letter is already filled in. Type the rest, or the whole word. Both work.
